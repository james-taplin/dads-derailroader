## Rules learned from individual locomotives

- **Driving wheels:** The stock definitions give each locomotive a nominal driver radius; the measured tread sits at that radius and the flange 14 to 40 mm above it.
- **Cab floors:** Some stock cabs have no visible floor mesh; the floor comes from the source collision hull.
- **Controls:** Every stock steam locomotive has three rotary controls (throttle, reverser, whistle); other functions are toggles.

The maintained details are in [review and geometry](https://github.com/james-taplin/dads-derailroader/blob/main/docs/review-and-geometry.md), [visible-surface placement](https://github.com/james-taplin/dads-derailroader/blob/main/docs/visible-surface-placement.md), and the [current validation notes](https://github.com/james-taplin/dads-derailroader/blob/main/docs/feature-roadmap.md).
