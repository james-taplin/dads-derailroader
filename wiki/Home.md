# derailroader wiki

<p align="center"><strong>From a locomotive in your Railroader Mods folder to a local Derail Valley CCL pack.</strong></p>

`derailroader` (`rr2dv` on the command line) reads a steam locomotive mod already installed on your computer, builds a Custom Car Loader pack, checks the exported bundle, and offers to install it in your Derail Valley `Mods` folder. It leaves the Railroader mod and your saves alone.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This is a work in progress. A successful build and bundle audit produce a **candidate**, not an accepted locomotive. Test it in game before treating it as finished.

The [repository README](https://github.com/james-taplin/derailroader#readme) is the short introduction and download guide. This wiki covers the details, including [Architecture](https://github.com/james-taplin/derailroader/wiki/Architecture) for contributors.

**Personal use:** Converted packs can contain third-party models, textures, and animations. `rr2dv` does not grant redistribution rights. Read [Personal use and provenance](https://github.com/james-taplin/derailroader/wiki/Personal-use-and-provenance) before installing or publishing anything.

Found a problem? Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/james-taplin/derailroader/issues) with the conversion stage, exact message, and the relevant `run.log` excerpt. Keep source assets and full private logs out of public posts.
