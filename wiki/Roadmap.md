# Roadmap

This page summarizes current implementation and validation status; it is not a release promise. See the [scope and validation notes](https://github.com/james-taplin/dads-derailroader/blob/main/docs/feature-roadmap.md) and [implementation notes](https://github.com/james-taplin/dads-derailroader/blob/main/docs/review-and-geometry.md) for evidence.

| Area | State | Evidence still needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supported stock steam locomotives | Build and audit pipeline implemented | In-game handling, controls, repeated use, and save/reload checks for each locomotive. |
| Vehicle review | Source facts, measurements, and assumptions are recorded | Confirm unresolved choices and retain evidence for changes. |
| Placement and interaction | Automated geometry and control checks are available | Confirm reach, travel, and operation in game. |
| Exported pack | Bundle audit checks structure and required controls | In-game acceptance; a passing audit alone does not establish it. |

A built or audited pack remains a candidate until [Testing a pack in Derail Valley](https://github.com/james-taplin/dads-derailroader/wiki/Testing-a-pack-in-Derail-Valley) is complete.
