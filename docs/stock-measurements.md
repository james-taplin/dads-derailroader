# Stock measurement findings

## Driving-wheel radius

The Railroader definition gives the nominal driving-wheel tread diameter. Across the 21 supported stock steam locomotives, the highest point of a rail-contacting wheel is above the nominal tread because of the flange; blind drivers have no flange. The probe therefore treats the nominal definition value as the tread candidate and records the measurement as supporting evidence, not as a replacement measurement.

## Cab-floor evidence

The collision hull on each locomotive has an upward-facing flat in or near the cab area. Some flats span the running board or catwalk, and some locomotives have multiple nearby levels. These readings are candidates for checking cab, bulb, and teleport placement; they are not currently used as build inputs.

## Source-model observations

Some source models contain zero-scale parts, unusually scaled meshes, shared truck packs, or names with trailing spaces. Keep these observations as per-model evidence and do not infer that the same geometry rule applies to every locomotive.
